About Us

We are the Church of Christ, a community of believers united by faith, love, and the teachings of Jesus Christ. Our doors are open to everyone — whether you are seeking answers, growing in your faith, or simply looking for a place to call home. We are committed to preaching the gospel in its purest form, worshipping God in spirit and in truth, and serving one another with love and compassion. Come as you are — you are welcome here.
